# Google Meet Intelligence Integration

## Objective

Extend the Communication Intelligence Layer beyond asynchronous messaging by incorporating synchronous customer interactions through Google Meet.

Every qualified appointment can optionally generate a Google Meet session using the organization's Google Workspace account.

The meeting becomes another first-class knowledge source for Continuous Discovery.

---

# Supported Capabilities

## Meeting Creation

The AI Platform can automatically:

- Create Google Meet sessions.
- Invite buyer and assigned broker.
- Attach the meeting to Google Calendar.
- Update CRM activities.
- Share meeting links through Chatwoot or WhatsApp.

---

## Meeting Artifacts

After the meeting, the platform ingests:

- Meeting metadata
- Duration
- Participants
- Recording (optional)
- Transcript (when available)
- AI-generated meeting notes
- Action items
- Follow-up tasks
