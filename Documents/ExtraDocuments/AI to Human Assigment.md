# ADR-004 — AI-First Conversation Ownership & Human Assignment Strategy

## Status

**Accepted**

---

# Decision

The platform adopts an **AI-First Conversation Ownership Model**.

Unlike the default Chatwoot workflow, where every new conversation is intended to be owned by a human agent, the AI Platform becomes the **initial owner** of every inbound lead.

However, **Chatwoot's native Assignment Engine will not be replaced**. Instead, the system delays human assignment until the AI has completed its qualification workflow.

This approach maximizes automation while preserving Chatwoot's mature assignment capabilities.

---

# Design Principles

- AI owns low-value repetitive work.
- Humans own high-value sales interactions.
- Reuse Chatwoot capabilities whenever possible.
- Do not fork or replace Chatwoot Assignment Engine.
- Keep ownership state explicit and auditable.
- Event-driven ownership transitions.

# Conversation Ownership Types

Every conversation has one active owner.

| Owner | Responsibility |
|---------|----------------|
| AI | Qualification, Q&A, Recommendation, Appointment Scheduling, Follow-ups |
| Human | Property Visit, Negotiation, Closing, Exceptional Cases |

Ownership may change multiple times during the customer journey.

# AI Workspace inside Chatwoot

The **AI Conversations** view contains conversations currently managed by the FastAPI AI Core.

# Human Visibility

Human agents always have read-only visibility while AI owns the conversation.

They may inspect:

- conversation history
- AI reasoning summary
- buyer profile
- CRM information
- recommendation results

without interrupting AI.

---

# Manual Takeover

A human agent may intervene at any time.

```
AI Owner

↓

Take Over

↓

Human Owner
```

Immediately after takeover:

- AI stops autonomous responses.
- Conversation ownership changes to Human.
- CRM is updated.

---

# Automatic Handoff

Once the AI successfully books an appointment:

```
Appointment Confirmed

↓

Release AI Ownership

↓

Move Conversation to Unassigned

↓

Trigger Assignment Engine

↓

Notify Human Brokers
```

At this point the AI no longer responds automatically.

---

# Human Assignment

The platform intentionally **reuses Chatwoot's native Assignment Engine**.

Supported assignment strategies include:

- Manual Claim
- Round Robin
- Team Assignment
- Load Balancing
- Availability-based Assignment

No custom assignment engine is implemented for the MVP.

---

# Notification Strategy

To minimize response delays after appointment booking, the AI Platform publishes a **Human Notification Event**.

Notification channels (configurable):

1. Chatwoot in-app notification.
2. Email.
3. Slack / Microsoft Teams.
4. WhatsApp notification.
5. Push notification (future).

Each notification contains:

- Buyer name
- Property
- Appointment date/time
- CRM stage
- Quick "Assign to Me" action

# Benefits

- AI-first customer engagement without replacing Chatwoot.
- Reuse of Chatwoot's proven Assignment Engine.
- Minimal modifications to the Chatwoot codebase.
- Clear ownership transitions between AI and humans.
- Faster broker response after appointment booking.
- Fully event-driven workflow.
- DDD-compliant ownership model.
- Easier upgrades to future Chatwoot releases.
- AI Platform remains reusable independently of the conversation platform.

---

# Future Evolution

The ownership model can later support:

- AI ↔ Human collaborative conversations.
- Multiple specialized AI agents working simultaneously.
- Skill-based broker assignment.
- Territory-aware assignment.
- AI-assisted broker copilots.
- Multi-office routing policies.
- Predictive broker assignment based on conversion history.

This evolution can be implemented without redesigning the ownership model, as Chatwoot continues acting as the communication platform while FastAPI remains the system of intelligence.
